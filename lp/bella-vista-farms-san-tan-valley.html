Questions

Does it cost me anything?

No. The builder pays the agent commission, and part of it comes back to you at closing. You do not pay us.

Will I still get the builder's incentives?

Yes. Builder incentives are separate. Your rebate comes from the agent commission, so you can stack both.

When do I get the money?

At closing. Your rebate is applied as a credit on your closing statement, usually toward closing costs or a rate buydown, as your lender allows.

Is a buyer rebate legal in Arizona?

Yes. Commission rebates are legal in Arizona when they are disclosed and approved by your lender, and they appear on your closing statement.

I already visited the model. Can I still get a rebate?

Maybe not with that builder, since most require your agent at the first visit. Text us anyway: other communities and builders are still open to you.
