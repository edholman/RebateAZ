Questions

Do I need my own agent?

No. You can buy directly with the listing team, who represents the seller. You are also welcome to bring your own agent.

How does the 3% seller credit work?

The seller credits 3% of the price at closing. You can use it for closing costs, prepaid items, or to buy down your interest rate, as your lender allows.

Do I have to use the at-cost financing?

No. You can use any lender. At-cost financing is an option that can let more of the credit go toward your rate.

Can I see the disclosures?

Yes. Ask for the seller's disclosure statement before you make an offer, and get your own home inspection.
