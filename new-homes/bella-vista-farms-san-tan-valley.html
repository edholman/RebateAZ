How to Get the Best Deal at Bella Vista Farms

  1. Target homes finishing this month or next. Builders carry the cost of every finished home they have not sold, so their strongest incentives, like rate buydowns and closing cost credits, usually go to quick move-in homes that can close within the next 30 to 60 days. Month end and quarter end are often when builders push hardest.
  2. Do not trust online listings alone. Many new build homes you see online are model homes or plans that are not built yet, not the actual inventory a builder is ready to deal on. The homes with real incentives are often finished or nearly finished homes that are listed inaccurately or not listed at all. Ask for the builder's current quick move-in list before you decide.
  3. Negotiate incentives, not the price. Builders rarely lower the base price, because every sale sets the comparable value for future appraisals and affects what earlier buyers in the community can resell for. Instead, builders usually negotiate on closing cost credits, upgrades and design center credits, and rate buydowns. That is where your leverage is.
  4. Register us before your first visit. Most builders will not pay an agent who was not registered at your first visit, and that registration is what makes your rebate possible.
  5. Stack the builder incentive with your rebate. Builder incentives often require the builder's affiliated lender, and every loan program limits total credits to the buyer. We help structure both so you keep as much as possible.
  6. Compare the full monthly payment. A lower rate or a bigger credit can be worth more than a lower price. Look at the rate, taxes, HOA, and total payment, not just the sticker price.

Where Bella Vista Farms Is

Bella Vista Farms sits in San Tan Valley, southeast of Queen Creek, next to the Central Arizona College San Tan campus. Gantzel Road and Hunt Highway are the main routes in, and both connect north toward Queen Creek, Mesa, and the Phoenix-Mesa Gateway airport area.

Nearby spots builders point to include San Tan Mountain Regional Park, Queen Creek Marketplace for shopping, and Schnepf Farms.

Builders, Amenities, and HOA

Several builders are selling in Bella Vista Farms right now, including Lennar, Mattamy Homes, Meritage Homes, and Dream Finders Homes. That gives you a real range of styles and price points in one community, and it gives you leverage: you can compare incentives across builders before you commit.

Builders describe community amenities including a pool, splash pad, large parks, playgrounds, basketball and volleyball courts, a soccer field, picnic areas, and walking paths. The community is governed by the Bella Vista Farms Community Association. Lennar lists an estimated HOA of about $114 per month; confirm current fees and any sub-association dues before you buy.

Schools

Bella Vista Farms is served by the Florence Unified School District. Builders commonly list Poston Butte High School, and some also mention nearby charter options.

Elementary school assignments can vary by address, so check the specific home on the Florence Unified boundary map before you buy. The district allows families to request other district schools, but buses only serve each home's assigned school.

San Tan Valley Is Now a Town

San Tan Valley voters approved incorporation in August 2025, and the Pinal County Board of Supervisors formally created the Town of San Tan Valley on September 17, 2025. Pinal County planned to keep providing services through June 30, 2026 while the new town took over.

If local services or future town taxes matter to you, confirm whether a specific address falls inside town limits, since a San Tan Valley mailing address alone does not guarantee it.

What to Know Before You Buy

Expect ongoing construction for a while, since multiple builders are still building. Prices across the community range widely, from the mid $300s with some builders to the upper $600s for Lennar's largest homes.

You may see the planned North-South Corridor freeway mentioned in marketing. According to ADOT, there is currently no construction timeline or funding identified for it, so do not count on it when judging a commute.

Some buyers ask about zero down USDA loans in this area. Eligibility depends on the exact address, so check it on the USDA eligibility map and confirm with your lender.
