How to Get the Best Deal at Asante

  1. Target homes finishing this month or next. Builders carry the cost of every finished home they have not sold, so their strongest incentives, like rate buydowns and closing cost credits, usually go to quick move-in homes that can close within the next 30 to 60 days. Month end and quarter end are often when builders push hardest.
  2. Do not trust online listings alone. Many new build homes you see online are model homes or plans that are not built yet, not the actual inventory a builder is ready to deal on. The homes with real incentives are often finished or nearly finished homes that are listed inaccurately or not listed at all. Ask for the builder's current quick move-in list before you decide.
  3. Negotiate incentives, not the price. Builders rarely lower the base price, because every sale sets the comparable value for future appraisals and affects what earlier buyers in the community can resell for. Instead, builders usually negotiate on closing cost credits, upgrades and design center credits, and rate buydowns. That is where your leverage is.
  4. Register us before your first visit. Most builders will not pay an agent who was not registered at your first visit, and that registration is what makes your rebate possible.
  5. Stack the builder incentive with your rebate. Builder incentives often require the builder's affiliated lender, and every loan program limits total credits to the buyer. We help structure both so you keep as much as possible.
  6. Compare the full monthly payment. A lower rate or a bigger credit can be worth more than a lower price. Look at the rate, taxes, HOA, and total payment, not just the sticker price.

Two Sides of Asante: All Ages and 55+

Artisan at Asante is the all-ages side, where Lennar, Pulte, and Taylor Morrison are building. Amenities include The Gallery at Asante Clubhouse, pools, parks, and trails, and Lennar adds pickleball courts, a fitness center, and sport courts.

Heritage at Asante is Lennar's gated active adult community for ages 55 and better, with its own clubhouse and fitness center, a resort-style pool and spa, the Heritage Links lighted putting course, pickleball, bocce, and dog parks. It has its own homeowners association, separate from the rest of Asante.

Tip: Lennar's 55+ collections at Asante carry the Heritage name, such as Heritage Cottage, Inspiration II, Encore II, and Freedom II. Everything else is open to all ages.

Where Asante Is

Asante is in north Surprise near 163rd Avenue, with Loop 303 access. Lennar's sales centers are at 24308 N 167th Lane for Artisan and 17382 W Chama Drive for Heritage.

Nearby favorites include White Tank Mountain Regional Park, the Surprise Aquatics Center, and the Surprise Tennis and Racquet Complex. A mixed-use development called Asante Trails is planned next to the community, with retail and a proposed hospital campus; timing has not been announced.

Schools

Asante is served by the Dysart Unified School District, and Asante Preparatory Academy is located inside the community. Lennar also lists Willow Canyon High School. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with Dysart before you buy.

HOA and Costs

Lennar lists an estimated HOA of about $185 per month for its Artisan homes. Heritage at Asante has a separate association with its own dues, so ask for the current amount if you are shopping the 55+ side.

Our Take on Asante

Asante gives you two very different options in one place: the all-ages Artisan side, with a new clubhouse and a school inside the community, and the gated Heritage side for 55+ buyers who want resort-style amenities.
