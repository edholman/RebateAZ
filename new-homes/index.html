How to Get the Best Deal on a New Build

  1. Target homes finishing this month or next. Builders carry the cost of every finished home they have not sold, so their strongest incentives, like rate buydowns and closing cost credits, usually go to quick move-in homes that can close within the next 30 to 60 days. Month end and quarter end are often when builders push hardest.
  2. Register an agent before your first visit. The builder's sales team works for the builder. Having your own agent costs you nothing, and registering first is what makes a rebate possible.
  3. Stack the builder incentive with your rebate. Builder incentives often require the builder's affiliated lender, and every loan program limits total credits to the buyer. We help structure both so you keep as much as possible.
  4. Compare the full monthly payment. A lower rate or a bigger credit can be worth more than a lower price. Look at the rate, taxes, HOA, and total payment, not just the sticker price.
