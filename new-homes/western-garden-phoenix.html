How to Get the Best Deal at Western Garden

  1. Target homes finishing this month or next. Builders carry the cost of every finished home they have not sold, so their strongest incentives, like rate buydowns and closing cost credits, usually go to quick move-in homes that can close within the next 30 to 60 days. Month end and quarter end are often when builders push hardest.
  2. Do not trust online listings alone. Many new build homes you see online are model homes or plans that are not built yet, not the actual inventory a builder is ready to deal on. The homes with real incentives are often finished or nearly finished homes that are listed inaccurately or not listed at all. Ask for the builder's current quick move-in list before you decide.
  3. Negotiate incentives, not the price. Builders rarely lower the base price, because every sale sets the comparable value for future appraisals and affects what earlier buyers in the community can resell for. Instead, builders usually negotiate on closing cost credits, upgrades and design center credits, and rate buydowns. That is where your leverage is.
  4. Register us before your first visit. Most builders will not pay an agent who was not registered at your first visit, and that registration is what makes your rebate possible.
  5. Stack the builder incentive with your rebate. Builder incentives often require the builder's affiliated lender, and every loan program limits total credits to the buyer. We help structure both so you keep as much as possible.
  6. Compare the full monthly payment. A lower rate or a bigger credit can be worth more than a lower price. Look at the rate, taxes, HOA, and total payment, not just the sticker price.

Where Western Garden Is

Western Garden is in west Phoenix near 97th Avenue, with the Lennar sales center at 3010 N 96th Lane. Lennar notes easy access to Loop 101 and Interstate 10, which connect you to the rest of the valley.

Nearby destinations include the Westgate Entertainment District, State Farm Stadium, and Wildlife World Zoo. Banner Estrella Medical Center on Thomas Road is close by as well.

Eight Collections in One Community

Lennar offers eight collections at Western Garden: Cottage, Crest, Reflection, Premier, Gateway, Discovery, Horizon, and Destiny. That range runs from compact homes under 1,000 square feet to plans over 3,000 square feet, so first time buyers and larger families can shop the same community.

The Horizon and Destiny collections include Lennar's Next Gen plans, which Lennar describes as a home within a home: an attached private suite with its own entrance, kitchenette, living area, bedroom, and bathroom. They are popular with multigenerational families and buyers who want space for a relative or long term guest.

Amenities and HOA

Lennar lists a community pool, sport courts including pickleball, a playground, and picnic areas. Lennar's published HOA estimate is about $199 per month. Confirm current fees and what they cover before you buy.

Schools

Western Garden is served by the Pendergast Elementary School District for kindergarten through eighth grade and the Tolleson Union High School District for high school. Lennar lists Villa de Paz Elementary and Westwind Elementary, plus Copper Canyon High School and Tolleson Union High School.

Because more than one school is listed at each level, assignments may vary by address. Confirm the specific home with each district before you buy.

Our Take on Western Garden

Western Garden puts you inside Phoenix city limits, with Loop 101, I-10, and Westgate close by instead of a long drive from the far suburbs. The spread of collections, from compact Cottage homes to Next Gen plans, means a first time buyer and a multigenerational family can both find a fit here.
