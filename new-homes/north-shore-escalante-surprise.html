How to Get the Best Deal at North Shore

  1. Compare new and resale. A resale North Shore home can come with upgrades, blinds and finished work already done, and no build wait. Compare it side by side with the builder's quick move-ins.
  2. Target homes finishing this month or next. Builders save their strongest incentives, like rate buydowns and closing cost credits, for finished homes that can close within 30 to 60 days.
  3. Negotiate incentives, not the price. Builders rarely cut the base price, because each sale sets the comparable value for future appraisals. They usually negotiate closing cost credits, upgrades and rate buydowns instead.
  4. Watch the preferred lender fine print. Fulton's incentives often require its preferred lender. Compare the rate, fees and total payment against an outside lender before you commit.
  5. Register us before your first visit. Most builders will not pay an agent who was not registered at your first visit, and that registration is what makes your rebate possible.

Amenities

Escalante has a large neighborhood aquatic center, pickleball courts, bocce ball, disc golf, walking trails, zip lines for kids, and wide open spaces. North Shore adds parks with ramadas, play structures, barbecues and greenbelts.

Fulton builds its homes to ENERGY STAR and Indoor airPLUS standards, and standard features include ceiling fans, faux wood blinds and two-tone paint.

Where North Shore Is

Escalante is at 163rd Avenue and Pinnacle Peak Road in north Surprise, close to Loop 303 and US 60 and a short drive to Lake Pleasant. The North Shore model homes are at 16236 W Charlotte Drive. Escalante sits near Asante; see our Asante guide to compare.

Schools

North Shore is in the Dysart Unified School District. Homes there have been assigned to Asante Preparatory Academy and Willow Canyon High School. Confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with Dysart before you buy.

HOA and Costs

HOA dues are about $149 per month. Expect one-time fees at closing as well, such as a transfer fee and a capital or reserve contribution. Ask for the current amounts before you write an offer.

Our Take on North Shore

North Shore is one of the few places in the northwest Valley where you can get a new two-story home with 6 or 7 bedrooms under about $725K. Big families and multigenerational buyers should look hard at the Arena Cove and Sea Ranch plans, and compare a finished resale against the builder's quick move-ins.

North Shore at Escalante FAQ

Who builds North Shore at Escalante?

Fulton Homes. North Shore is one of four Fulton neighborhoods in Escalante, along with Redwood Valley, Silverado and Sonoma Coast.

How much are homes at North Shore at Escalante?

Fulton lists North Shore plans starting from $514,900 for the Rockaway up to $634,900 for the Sea Ranch. Finished quick move-in homes with lot premiums and upgrades often list higher, in the $600s and $700s.

What floor plans are at North Shore?

Rockaway, Harris Beach and Pelican Bay are single story, from 2,165 to 2,357 square feet. Pelican Bay with Loft, Arena Cove and Sea Ranch are two story, from 3,204 to 3,857 square feet with up to 7 bedrooms.

What schools serve North Shore at Escalante?

North Shore is in the Dysart Unified School District. Homes there have been assigned to Asante Preparatory Academy and Willow Canyon High School. Confirm assignments for a specific address with the district.

What are the HOA fees at North Shore at Escalante?

About $149 per month. Confirm the current amount and any transfer or capital fees with the HOA before you buy.

Is there a resale option at North Shore?

Sometimes. A resale can mean no build wait and upgrades already done. Ask us about current resale homes in North Shore along with the builder's quick move-ins.
