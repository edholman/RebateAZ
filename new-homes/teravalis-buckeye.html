Community guide updated September 2026.

Its first village, Floreo, opened in November 2025 with seven builders, and Lennar's published prices there start in the low $300s. Where Teravalis Is

Teravalis sits in north Buckeye between the White Tank and Belmont mountains. Floreo, the first village, is at Sun Valley Parkway and Teravalis Parkway, more than 10 miles north of Interstate 10. Plan on driving for most shopping and services for now; a grocery store and medical facilities are planned within the community.

At full build out, Howard Hughes plans Teravalis to cover about 37,000 acres, with thousands of acres of natural open space. Expect construction around you for years.

Parks and the Echo Community Center

Floreo includes Welcome Park, Floreo Park, and Windrose Park, plus trails. The centerpiece Echo Community Center, a 22,000 square foot amenity center with a large pickleball pavilion, a water park, and a fitness center, broke ground in September 2025 and is expected to open in early 2027.

Water

Water is a fair question anywhere in Buckeye. Floreo, where homes are selling now, holds Certificates of Assured Water Supply from the state, which confirm a 100-year water supply for that village. Water for future villages beyond Floreo is still being worked out.

Taxes, HOA, and Schools

Lennar lists an approximate property tax rate of about 1.25%, noticeably higher than many other valley communities. Newer master plans often include special taxing districts that fund roads and infrastructure, so ask for an exact tax estimate on any home before you commit. Lennar's HOA estimate is about $184 per month.

Schools are planned inside Teravalis, but none are open yet. Confirm the assigned school district and schools for a specific address before you buy.

Our Take on Teravalis

Teravalis offers some of the lower new home prices in the valley, which makes it worth a look for buyers stretching their budget. Weigh that against the higher property tax rate, the drive to I-10, and amenities that are still being built, and compare the full monthly payment, not just the price, before you decide.
