North Phoenix Home Buyer Rebate Guide: Desert Ridge, Norterra and Stetson Valley (2026)

Updated November 2026 • 7 min read

Short answer: North Phoenix homes sold for a median of $619,995 over the past year, and nearly 4 in 10 sold for $700,000 or more. At those prices a buyer commission rebate is real money: on a $650,000 home where the seller offers your agent 3%, $9,750 comes back to you at closing.

How the rebate works:
Your agent keeps 1.5% of the price (minimum $4,500). Everything the seller or builder offers your agent above that is credited to you at closing, up to 2% of the price. Full service, no extra cost.

Where North Phoenix Buyers Are Looking

  • Desert Ridge (85050, 85054): Close to the 101 and the 51, Desert Ridge Marketplace, and the Mayo Clinic campus. A large share of 85054 sales are condos and townhomes, which pulls the median down while single-family homes often run $700,000 and up.
  • Norterra and Sonoran Foothills (85085): Newer master-planned neighborhoods along I-17, close to the new TSMC chip plant, which has added steady demand on the north side.
  • Stetson Valley (85083): West of I-17 with larger lots and mountain views, mostly single-family homes.
  • North-central Phoenix (85024): Established neighborhoods at lower price points, a good fit for buyers who want North Phoenix schools and commutes for less.

Schools on the north side are mostly in the Deer Valley Unified and Paradise Valley Unified districts. Check the boundary for any specific address before you make an offer.

New Construction in North Phoenix

Builders on the north side often offer higher agent commissions than resale sellers, which is where full 2% rebates are most common. Bring your agent, or have them register you, on your first visit to any builder. Builders usually will not pay an agent who was not registered at that first visit. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the median home price in North Phoenix?

$619,995 across zip codes 85024, 85050, 85054, 85083 and 85085, based on 1,872 sales from September 2025 through August 2026. Half of sales fell between $482,750 and $818,089.

How much is a buyer rebate on a $700,000 North Phoenix home?

If the seller offers your agent 3%, your agent keeps 1.5% and you get $10,500 back at closing. At 3.5%, you get the full 2%, or $14,000.

Can I use a rebate with a jumbo loan?

Usually yes. Jumbo lenders set their own limits on closing credits, and most allow a credit that covers closing costs. Tell your lender about the rebate at preapproval so it is planned in.

Is a buyer rebate legal in Phoenix?

Yes. Arizona allows rebates when they are disclosed, written into your buyer agreement, and shown on your closing statement. See Is a Realtor Rebate Legal in Arizona?
