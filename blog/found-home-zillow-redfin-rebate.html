Found a Home on Zillow or Redfin? You Can Still Get a Buyer Rebate

Updated November 2026 • 3 min read

Short answer: yes. Zillow, Redfin and Realtor.com show homes listed by many brokerages, and you can buy any of them with the agent you choose, including a rebate agent. The one thing to watch: the "request a tour" or "contact agent" buttons often connect you with an agent the site picks, who may ask you to sign a buyer agreement before the tour.

Should I Call the Listing Agent?

The listing agent works for the seller. Calling them can be useful for a quick question, but if they show you the home and write your offer, they are representing the seller's interests too, and there is no rebate for you in that arrangement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use any agent for a home I found on Zillow?

Yes. You choose your agent. The site's contact buttons are one way to reach an agent, not a requirement.

Does the home have to be listed with a certain brokerage to get a rebate?

No. The rebate comes from the commission the seller or builder offers your agent, whoever listed the home.

How fast can you show me a home I found online?

Often the same or next day, depending on the seller's showing instructions. Text us the address.
