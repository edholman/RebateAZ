Questions

How many zip codes are in Maricopa County?

This map shows the 139 Maricopa County zip codes that cover neighborhoods where people live, from the county's USPS zip code file. PO box and single-address zip codes (post office boxes, Arizona State University, large mailers) are left off because no homes use them as a street address.

Some of these also cover parts of neighboring towns. PO box zip codes are not included.

Do zip codes follow city limits?

No. Zip codes are mail delivery routes set by the Postal Service, not city boundaries. A home can have a Phoenix mailing address and sit in another city, or the reverse. Check the county assessor or the city's own map when city limits matter, for example for taxes, schools or permits.

Where does this map's data come from?

The outlines come from Maricopa County's USPS zip code boundary file, published by Maricopa County GIS. Home prices come from Maricopa County Recorder sales records (affidavits of property value) from September 2025 through August 2026. Outlines are simplified slightly so the map loads fast on a phone.

Sources: Maricopa County GIS, USPS zip code boundaries (Maricopa County does not guarantee the data is complete or current). Home prices: Maricopa County Recorder affidavits of property value, September 2025 to August 2026; see how we research our numbers and the full home prices by zip code tables.
