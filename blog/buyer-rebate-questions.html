Getting started

  • How does a buyer rebate work in Arizona?
    A licensed agent represents you, and part of the commission the seller or builder pays comes back to you at closing.
  • Is a realtor rebate legal in Arizona?
    Yes, when it is disclosed, written into your buyer agreement and paid through escrow.
  • How and when is the rebate paid?
    As a credit through escrow on the day you close, shown on your Closing Disclosure.
  • Is a realtor rebate taxable?
    Generally no. The IRS has treated it as a reduction of the purchase price, which lowers your cost basis.

Your situation

  • I found a home on Zillow or Redfin. Can I still get a rebate?
    Yes. You choose your agent. Text us the address before you click Request a Tour.
  • I am already working with an agent. Can I switch?
    Usually, once you check your buyer agreement for its term, cancellation terms and protection clause.
  • My lender referred me. Can the agent still give me a credit?
    Yes. RESPA bans the agent and lender paying each other for referrals, not a disclosed credit to you.
  • What if the seller won't pay my agent?
    Ask for it in your offer, ask for a lower price instead, or pay the fee yourself. Your agent tells you before you tour.

New construction

  • I already visited a model home alone. Now what?
    Ask the builder its policy before you sign, and register before visiting any other community.
  • Can I get a rebate on a new build?
    Yes, if your agent is registered on your first visit. Builders usually pay a full commission.

Money and loans

  • Can I use a rebate with FHA, VA or a conventional loan?
    Yes, for closing costs, prepaids and points, within each loan's limit on credits. Not for the down payment.
  • How much is the rebate on a $400,000 home?
    $6,000 when the seller offers your agent 3%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a buyer commission rebate?

Part of the commission paid to your buyer's agent, credited back to you at closing. With RebateAZ, it is the commission offered to your agent minus 1.5% of the price (minimum $4,500), up to 2% of the price.

Who pays for a buyer rebate?

It comes out of the commission the seller or builder already offers your agent. It is not an extra cost to the seller.

Do I still get full service with a rebate?

Yes. With RebateAZ you get a licensed agent for showings, comps, your offer, inspections, negotiation and closing, with no per-showing or upfront fees.
