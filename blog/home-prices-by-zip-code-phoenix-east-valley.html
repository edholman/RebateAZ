How to Read These Numbers

  • Paid cash: the share of buyers who reported no loan on the county affidavit. Cash offers close faster and skip the appraisal, so a high share means a financed offer needs to be stronger on price or terms.
  • Bought by a company: the share of homes deeded to an LLC, corporation or partnership. This includes investors, flippers and iBuyers, and also some families who buy through an LLC.
  • FHA or VA: a high share means sellers in that zip code are used to these loans, which helps if you are using one.
  • 1-year change in median: the median for September 2025 to August 2026 compared with the year before. It moves with the mix of homes sold, such as more new builds or more luxury sales, so it is not a measure of what any one home gained or lost.
  • Rebate at median: what comes back to you at closing on a home at that zip code's median price when the seller offers your agent 3%.

How We Got These Numbers

We used Maricopa County Recorder affidavits of property value for single-family and condo sales from September 2025 through August 2026, excluding sales under $100,000, multi-parcel sales and land. How the buyer paid comes from the affidavit. Company buyers are identified from the buyer's name on the deed. We report medians, not averages, and only zip codes with at least 60 sales.
