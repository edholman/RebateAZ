Questions

Are home prices going down in Phoenix?

Across the 124 Phoenix-area zip codes on this map, the median sale price was $475,000 from September 2025 through August 2026. Counting resales only (new builds left out, so a wave of builder sales does not skew it), the median was $467,000, versus $472,500 the year before (-1.2%). Prices were not falling everywhere: 26 zip codes saw the resale median rise at least 1%, 82 fell at least 1%, and 16 were roughly flat.

Which Phoenix zip codes had the biggest price increases?

Among zip codes with at least 100 resales, the largest 1-year increases in median resale price were 85018 (Phoenix, +20.4%), 85003 (Phoenix, +12.8%), 85258 (Scottsdale, +11.6%), 85259 (Scottsdale, +11.0%), 85266 (Scottsdale, +9.3%). New builds are left out, but the median still reflects which homes resold, so check the sales count and look at individual comps before reading too much into one zip.

Where do cash buyers buy the most in the Phoenix area?

The highest shares of cash purchases among zip codes with at least 100 sales were 85263 (Rio Verde, 58%), 85262 (Scottsdale, 50%), 85377 (Carefree, 50%), 85266 (Scottsdale, 49%), 85375 (Sun City West, 49%). Retirement communities and investor-heavy areas lead. In those zip codes, financed buyers often compete with cash offers.

Which zip codes have the most investor or LLC buyers?

Homes bought by a company (an LLC, corporation or similar name on the deed) were most common in 85253 (Paradise Valley, 31%), 85251 (Scottsdale, 27%), 85254 (Scottsdale, 24%), 85017 (Phoenix, 23%), 85018 (Phoenix, 23%), among zip codes with at least 100 sales.

Where do the most FHA buyers buy?

FHA loans were the largest share of sales in 85326 (Buckeye, 52%), 85353 (Tolleson, 50%), 85323 (Avondale, 49%), 85354 (Tonopah, 44%), 85037 (Phoenix, 41%). A high FHA share usually means sellers there are used to FHA offers and lower down payments.

Where does this data come from?

Every number comes from Maricopa County Recorder affidavits of property value for single family homes, condos and townhomes sold for $100,000 to $10 million, September 2025 through August 2026, compared with the prior 12 months. Price change compares resales only: homes sold by builders are left out so new construction does not skew it. Zip codes need at least 30 sales to appear. Company buyers are deeds where the buyer's name is an LLC, corporation or similar business name.
