Buyer Rebates on $700,000, $800,000 and $1 Million Homes in Arizona (2026)

Updated November 2026 • 6 min read

Short answer: on a $800,000 Arizona home where the seller offers your agent 3%, a RebateAZ buyer gets $12,000 back at closing. At 3.5%, it is the full 2%: $16,000. On a $1 million home, the rebate runs $10,000 to $20,000.

The formula:
Your rebate = the commission offered to your agent − 1.5% of the price (minimum $4,500), up to 2% of the price. You get full service from a licensed agent either way.

How the Rebate Works at Higher Prices

  • Conventional loans up to $832,750: that is the 2026 conforming loan limit in Maricopa County. Credits from the seller and your agent are capped at 3% to 9% of the price depending on your down payment, so a rebate fits easily. Details in our guide to rebates with each loan type.
  • Jumbo loans above $832,750: jumbo lenders set their own rules on closing credits. Most allow a credit toward closing costs and rate buydowns. Tell your lender about the rebate at preapproval.
  • Cash purchases: about 33% of $1 million to $1.5 million buyers paid cash. With no lender, the rebate is simply credited on your settlement statement.
  • Buying down your rate: on a larger loan, putting the rebate toward points can lower your payment for the life of the loan. Ask your lender to price both options.

Where the Rebate Goes Furthest

The largest rebates come where prices are high and sellers or builders offer full commissions: Scottsdale, North Phoenix, Gilbert, east Mesa and south Chandler, plus new construction anywhere in the Valley. See our local guides for North Phoenix, Scottsdale, Gilbert, Chandler and Mesa.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a buyer rebate on a $700,000 home in Arizona?

$7,000 to $14,000, depending on whether the seller offers your agent 2.5%, 3%, or 3.5%. Your agent keeps 1.5% and the rest comes back to you, up to 2%.

How much is a buyer rebate on a $1 million home?

$10,000 if the seller offers 2.5%, $15,000 at 3%, and $20,000 at 3.5%.

Can I use a rebate with a jumbo loan?

Usually yes. Jumbo lenders set their own limits on closing credits, and most allow a credit for closing costs or points. Tell your lender about it at preapproval.

Do I still get full service on a luxury purchase?

Yes. You work with a licensed agent for showings, pricing, negotiation, inspections and closing. The only difference is that part of the commission comes back to you.
